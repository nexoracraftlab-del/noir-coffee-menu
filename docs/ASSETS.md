14 source photographs were downloaded from Pexels on 2026-10-16 and converted locally to WebP. The complete per-file record (source page, original image URL, credit when verified, license URL and access date) is in [image-sources.json](image-sources.json).

License: [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Pexels permits free personal and commercial use subject to its license restrictions. No endorsement by a photographer, café or Pexels is claimed. Generic credits marked “Pexels contributor” should be resolved from the linked photo page if explicit photographer attribution is added later.

Processing: center crop to 5:4 at 480×384 and 800×640, WebP quality 82. `hero.webp` is derived from photo 312418 at 1600×1000, quality 85. The same photo can illustrate related sample products; serving differences are disclosed in the product sheet. The crop does not imply that the depicted business is NOIR.
